Best strategy for fine-tuning Transformer classifiers (sentiment/topic) on your kind of data:

Data first
Clean obvious artefacts (prefix/suffix, metadata), normalize labels, deduplicate near-duplicates.
Split by document ID/source/time if possible (avoid train/test leakage by duplicates).
Use 3 splits (always)
Train: most data
Validation: small but representative (2k–10k is enough) used for early stopping/model selection
Test: fixed, never touched until the end
Balance what matters
If you care about dialect, ensure validation is MSA vs Dialect balanced, and training batches aren’t dominated by one variant (oversample minority or downsample majority).
Two-stage training (best when labels are noisy / mixed quality)
Stage A: train on the big 300k (even if weak/noisy labels) for 1–2 epochs.
Stage B: continue training (“fine-tune the fine-tune”) on a smaller high-quality curated set (manual or high-confidence) with lower LR.
Stable training recipe
Start with max_len 256, 2 epochs, LR ~2e-5, fp16, grad accumulation, gradient checkpointing.
Track macro-F1 (not just accuracy), and per-group metrics (MSA/Dialect).
Pick the right base
Topic: start from an NLI-trained backbone (good semantics), then train a normal 18-class head.
Sentiment: start from a sentiment-pretrained backbone (like you did), then adapt to your domain/dialect.
